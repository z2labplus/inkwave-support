开始游戏 / Getting started

横屏进入游戏，选择场地、武器和难度。左摇杆移动，右侧空白区域滑动瞄准，按住射击；潜入己方墨水可以补墨。设置可调整画质、音量、语言和瞄准灵敏度。

Play in landscape. Choose an arena, weapon and difficulty. Move with the left stick, drag the right side to aim, and hold Fire. Swim in your team's ink to refill. Settings includes graphics, audio, language and aiming sensitivity.

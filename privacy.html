隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 27 日

离线游戏

彩墨小队是由 yushen zhang 提供的单人离线游戏，队友和对手由电脑控制。无需创建账号，没有广告、应用内购买、第三方统计或跨应用追踪。

我们不收集游戏数据

游戏不会向开发者或第三方发送玩家资料、对局记录、设备标识、位置或使用行为。游戏不申请相机、麦克风、照片、联系人或定位权限。

仅在设备上保存

昵称、等级、经验、战绩、武器与外观选择、语言和设置保存在本机的应用数据中，不用于公开排行榜，也不由开发者同步到服务器。系统备份由你的 Apple 设备设置控制。

删除本地数据

在系统设置中删除 App 可移除当前设备上的应用数据；“卸载 App”可能保留数据。Apple 备份可在系统设置中另外管理。重新安装或删除数据可能导致进度丢失。

Apple 服务

下载付款由 Apple App Store 处理，开发者不会收到你的付款卡资料。Apple 可能按其隐私政策及你选择的系统设置处理购买、诊断和商店统计数据。

支持联系

如主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到你提供的邮箱地址和邮件内容，仅用于答复与处理问题，不作广告用途。你可以请求删除支持邮件；请勿发送密码或付款资料。

联系方式

开发者：yushen zhang。支持邮箱：telegram2ai@gmail.com。本政策适用于彩墨小队 1.0.0；如数据处理方式变化，会同步更新政策和商店隐私申报。

Privacy Policy

Updated September 27, 2026

Offline play

Splash Squad is a single-player offline game provided by yushen zhang. Teammates and opponents are computer controlled. No account is required. The game has no ads, in-app purchases, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

No gameplay data collection

The game does not send player profiles, match history, device identifiers, location or usage activity to the developer or third parties. It does not request camera, microphone, photo, contact or location access.

On-device storage

Your nickname, level, experience, match statistics, equipment, appearance, language and settings are stored in local app data. They are not used for public leaderboards or synced to a developer server. Device backups are controlled by your Apple device settings.

Deleting local data

Deleting the app in system settings removes its data from the current device; offloading may retain data. Apple backups can be managed separately in system settings. Removing data or reinstalling may lose your progress.

Apple services

Apple handles payment for the app download. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may process purchases, diagnostics and store statistics under its own privacy policy and your system settings.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to provide, only to respond and resolve the issue, not for advertising. You may request deletion of support correspondence. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Contact

Developer: yushen zhang. Support: telegram2ai@gmail.com. This policy covers Splash Squad 1.0.0. Changes to data practices will be reflected in this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure.
